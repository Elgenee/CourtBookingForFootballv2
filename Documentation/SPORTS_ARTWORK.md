# Homepage sports artwork

Updated the "Five sports, one pitch" cards to use smaller, realistic equipment cutouts inspired by the supplied banner.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then exported as transparent 320 × 320 WebP images for the website. Display size: 84 × 84 CSS pixels inside 104px background circles. Original SVGs remain available.

## Final prompt set

Shared prompt used for each asset:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: small equipment illustration for a sports booking website card. Photorealistic premium studio product cutout, soft warm lighting from upper left, realistic surface texture and three-dimensional volume, clean silhouette and subtle contact shadow, centered on a genuinely transparent background. Square canvas, object fully visible filling about 75% of width and height. Slight elevated three-quarter view. No text, no branding, no watermark, no frame, no background disc, no decorative lines. Designed to remain legible at 76px.

### football

Subject: One classic black-and-white association football (soccer ball), accurate pentagonal and hexagonal stitched panels, softly textured white synthetic leather, black panels, round spherical shape.

### flag-football

Subject: One brown American football lying diagonally bottom-left to top-right, white laces along the top and white stripe near both pointed ends, realistic pebbled brown leather.

### frisbee

Subject: One white ultimate frisbee disc tilted toward the viewer, visible rounded rim, thin orange concentric accent ring on the upper face. Realistic molded matte plastic and subtle radial rim grooves.

### rugby

Subject: One white oval rugby ball with curved dark forest-green and black panels, diagonally tilted bottom-left to top-right, distinct elongated oval rugby shape, realistic grippy textured surface, no laces.

### cricket

Subject: One pale natural willow cricket bat leaning diagonally with its black rubber handle toward upper left and its flat wooden blade toward lower right, with one small polished deep-red cricket ball at the foot in the foreground, visible stitched white seam. Entire bat and ball fully visible together.
